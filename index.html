    What I bring to your city

    Four basic formats, each of which can customized, or combined into a weekend or longer retreat. I am also glad to join an existing retreat or to co-design a new one with your team.

    • Concerts

      Hindustaanee classical music presented as part of the Naadaalay concert series, from intimate house gatherings to full evening programmes. Each raag is introduced in plain language so that listeners new to the tradition can follow it as closely as experienced ones.

    • Workshops

      Hands-on sessions built on naad yog, the practice of using sound and attentive listening as a path of inner work. Open to people with no musical training as well as to practising musicians.

    • Sound baths

      Natural voice, Sacred Sounds of Sanskrit, traditional Hindustaanee classical drone, and other instruments as suitable.

    • One-on-one sessions

      Lessons in raag music, basic singing, natural voice, improvisation on your own instrument. Naad yog fundamentals, Sanskrit phonemes for better mantra chanting. Private sessions for somatic sound, musical meditation, sound healing, body work, private sound bath.

    About me

    I am Ashish Dha, a raag musician and naad yog practitioner based in Pune, India. My work moves between the concert stage, the workshop room and the retreat space. I invite people to PLAY with music, sound, and energy.

    I have been performing music for over two decades, teaching for over one. I have carried this work to audiences in three different continents, and have had multi-national tours in Europe every year since 2023. Over the years those journeys have grown into a wide international circle of organisers, teachers, artists and friends, and many of the invitations I receive now come through them.

    Alongside performing, I study the structure of raag itself. I am building an open database of Hindustaanee raags and their parent scales called thaaTs, and have built custom software for exploring the 'mysterious world of microtones' — both projects combine my musical training and my programming passion.

      Naadaalay DB

      A free reference database of Hindustaanee raags, showcasing a new rigorously scientific approach to South Asian classical music. It starts from all 4,096 possible scales within the octave and describes each raag through its ascent and descent, thaat, omitted notes, symmetry, consonance and more, so that a student, teacher or curious listener can search the tradition by its musical structural features.
