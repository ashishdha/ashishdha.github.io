    What I bring to your city

    Three formats, each of which can stand alone or be combined into a weekend.

    • Concerts

      Hindustaanee classical music presented as part of the Naadaalay concert series, from intimate house gatherings to full evening programmes. Each raag is introduced in plain language so that listeners new to the tradition can follow it as closely as experienced ones.

    • Workshops

      Hands-on sessions built on naad yog, the practice of using sound and attentive listening as a path of inner work. Open to people with no musical training as well as to practising musicians.

    • Retreats and collaborations

      Creative and somatic retreat work, developed together with partners in Europe and India. I am glad to join an existing retreat or to co-design a new one with your team.

    About me

    I am Ashish Dha, a musician and naad yog practitioner based in Pune, India. My work moves between the concert stage, the workshop room and the retreat space, and it rests on one conviction: sound, listened to closely, is a way of practising.

    I run the Naadaalay concert series, and I have carried this work to audiences in Europe on three earlier tours. Over the years those journeys have grown into a wide international circle of organisers, teachers, artists and friends, and many of the invitations I receive now come through them.

    Alongside performing, I study the structure of raag itself. I built an open database of Hindustaanee scales and raags, a project that sits where my musical training meets my programming, and I bring the same curiosity to the stage.

    Naadaalay DB

    A free reference database of Hindustaanee raags that I designed and built myself. It starts from all 4,096 possible scales within the octave and describes each raag through its ascent and descent, thaat, omitted notes, symmetry, consonance and more, so that a student, teacher or curious listener can search the tradition by its musical structure.
